New text

Chinese makers rarely attacked incumbents head on. They entered where incumbents were thin: a specialist product, a new group of customers, or a change of technology.

Learning 1. Enter through a narrow beachhead, then broaden.

SANY began building in India at Chakan, near Pune, but it did not start in the core of the excavator market. It went after long-reach excavators, a specialist machine used to clean canals, dig wells and build metro lines. Within about 18 months it had 52% of that niche nationally and 62% in Maharashtra, figures it announced in October 2016.21

The niche mattered less for its size than for what it forced SANY to build: dealers, service engineers and parts depots, which could then sell everything else. By 2025 SANY India claimed 60% of India's piling rigs, 55% of its cranes, 50% of its port machinery and 20% of its excavators. These are company claims, but trade data and India's crane case support their direction.22 By September 2026 it reported about 42 dealers and more than 45,000 machines delivered across India and South Asia.98

In Indonesia the beachhead was a customer group rather than a product: the companies building nickel smelters and mines, many of them Chinese-owned. Hitachi's distributor dates aggressive Chinese competition to 2021, as nickel investment accelerated.82,84 Following Chinese customers abroad is now the main route into the machines factories buy (Chapter 3).

Where it stops. A beachhead is hardest to hold where machines work hard and resale value matters. Tata Hitachi's share of Indian mining excavators fell from about 50% to 20% as Chinese makers entered, then recovered to about 35%, even as the number of competitors doubled to about eight.42

What others can copy. Choose a niche for the channel it builds, not only for its size. Incumbents should look hardest at the segments where their own range is thin, because that is where the next entrant will land. Dealers can add a challenger brand in a niche without undercutting their main franchise.

Learning 6. Use electrification as the entry wedge.

A change of technology lowers the cost of switching brands. Chinese forklift makers arrived just as buyers moved to electric machines. In 2024 China supplied 60–69% of electric-forklift imports in India, Indonesia and Thailand; in Thailand Japan's share fell to 13.6%.10–12 The Chinese makers' own sales lead with electric: 70.15% of Heli's 2025 unit sales were electric.36 Behind this sits battery cost. BloombergNEF put the average battery pack at US$108 per kWh in 2025, and packs made in China at US$84.132

Where it stops. Construction equipment is only at the start of the same change. In China electric loaders reached 17.1% of loader sales in early 2025, but electric excavators stayed at about 0.08%.133 Of 73,295 excavators China exported in the first half of 2026, 135 were electric.53 Chinese firms are positioning early all the same: XCMG's Weda Bay plant in Indonesia makes electric loaders, LiuGong's new Karawang plant will make only electric machines, and SANY India launched an electric excavator and loader in 2026.29,30,98

What others can copy. Construction-equipment incumbents still have a window. The cheapest moment to win a customer is when the technology changes, so the next generation should launch then, with battery supply secured early. Forklift incumbents can go further and source a value range from a Chinese maker, as Jungheinrich has done (Chapter 4).

Learning 2. Treat customer credit as part of the product.

Many equipment buyers in India and Indonesia are small contractors and miners who struggle to borrow from banks. For them the down payment and the monthly instalment matter more than the list price. Tata Hitachi's managing director said in September 2026 that first-time buyers in road construction were turning to non-bank lenders, and that Chinese makers had gained ground with easier financing.42

SANY's path in Indonesia shows the move in full (Exhibit 3). In December 2017 its official distributor, PT Sany Perkasa, signed a memorandum of understanding with MNC Leasing on equipment finance, and a year later the two launched low-down-payment loans.92,103,104 In September 2025 SANY signed an agreement worth about RMB1.45bn with two finance companies.106 On 14 September 2026 Indonesia's financial regulator licensed SANY's own finance company, PT Sany Indonesia Finance.23 In the same month XCMG and its dealer launched ABC Multifinance, a joint venture that finances a machine across its purchase, maintenance, use and resale.56,110 Local lenders now treat Chinese brands as normal credit risk: one of SANY's 2025 finance partners belongs to the group of Trakindo, Indonesia's Caterpillar dealer.108,109

Credit also runs between related companies. In India's crane case the trade regulator found that XCMG's exporter gave its related Indian importer credit of up to three years, which it called "not normal", and that XCMG had misreported the terms. The regulator took the credit into account when it calculated export prices.14

Where it stops. Share bought on credit is exposed in a downturn, and the 2026–27 slowdown will test it (Chapter 1). Credit between related companies is also hard for regulators to see through, which invites scrutiny in trade cases.

What others can copy. Incumbents with their own finance arms can copy this move most cheaply: design the down payment, loan term, seasonal repayments and trade-in terms together with the machine, and aim them at first-time buyers. Hitachi's Indonesian distributor already lists financing through its own finance arm as one of its answers to Chinese competition.82 But the public record shows little sign of incumbents aiming their finance at the small buyers Chinese makers have won.

Learning 3. Price just enough, then invest in the lifecycle.

The price gap is smaller than commonly assumed. The only regulator-checked comparison in these markets found that Chinese cranes undercut Indian prices by 0–10% between April 2023 and March 2024. What hurt the Indian producer was a squeeze: Chinese landed prices sat below its cost of sales.14 Asking prices point the same way. On Indonesia's public e-catalogue, a SANY 21-tonne excavator was listed at Rp2.10bn, against Rp2.25bn for Komatsu's equivalent, both including tax.N40 The gap was wider earlier: in 2021, as SANY's Indonesian share reached 21% against Komatsu's 22%, it had cut prices by about 20%.99 The pattern is a gap large enough to matter, but not so large that buyers see a different class of machine.

The money then goes into the machine's life. This is where incumbents are strongest. In the first half of 2026 United Tractors' heavy-equipment revenue fell 28%, yet its revenue from spare parts and maintenance rose 1% to Rp5.5 trillion, about 37% of the total. It supports that business with 40 workshops and remanufacturing sites and 117 warehouses.89,118 Chinese makers are now building the same. On 16 September 2026 XCMG broke ground in Balikpapan on its first overseas base for overhauling and remanufacturing mining-machine parts. Its aftermarket revenue grew 33.23% in the first half of 2025, four times as fast as the company.24,119 XCMG's Indonesian service policy promises a response within 15 minutes and parts within 72 hours, and SANY India lists more than 120 service branches.120,121

Where it stops. Resale value is unresolved. Tata Hitachi's managing director says customers find Chinese machines have "no resale price" and fail more often.42 That is an incumbent's claim, and no free public data on used prices by brand exist to test it. Incumbents also lead on machine data: Komatsu tracks about 840,000 machines through its Komtrax system, and no Chinese maker publishes a comparable figure.123

What others can copy. Incumbents should treat parts, service and remanufacturing as a revenue line to grow, and publish evidence on uptime and resale value before a competitor or a lender defines it. Dealers can sell parts, service and trade-in as one bundle.

Learning 4. Localise plants, then export from the host.

Four Chinese makers build in India. SANY has invested more than INR 1,000 crore in its plant at Chakan, and SANY India shipped about 1,200 machines abroad in 2024. XCMG has made machines in Chennai since 2021, Zoomlion opened an INR 500 crore plant at Khed City in May 2024, and LiuGong opened an INR 272 crore expansion at Pithampur in July 2026.22,25–27,98

Since November 2025 Chinese makers have opened or broken ground on five plants and service bases in Indonesia and Thailand (Exhibit 4):

  • Heli's forklift and battery joint venture in Rayong, Thailand (November 2025), planned for 10,000 forklifts and 10,000 battery sets a year;32
  • Hangcha's plant in Chonburi, Thailand (opened June 2026);31
  • LiuGong's US$317m plant at Karawang, Indonesia, for 5,000 electric machines a year (July 2026);28,29
  • XCMG's Weda Bay plant, "XCMG's first overseas new-energy factory", which began making electric loaders in July 2026;30
  • XCMG's remanufacturing base in Balikpapan (September 2026).24

Three reasons recur. A local plant opens public purchasing under local-content rules (Learning 5). It insures against tariffs and trade cases aimed at Chinese-made goods, such as the UK's duties of 18.81–40.08% on Chinese excavators.43 And it puts the maker next to its customers: XCMG's Weda Bay plant sits in the industrial park where its first customer, Tsingshan, operates.30 The host then becomes an export base. LiuGong calls Karawang its production base for the Asia-Pacific before it expands exports to North America and Europe.28,29

Where it stops. Plant economics depend on trade policy, which can change at a stroke. And an announced plant is not always a production plant: India's trade regulator did not accept Haitian's Indian unit as a domestic producer (Chapter 3).

What others can copy. Most incumbents already own plants in the region; Volvo will launch wheel loaders built in India in 2027.101 The open question is whether incumbents use those plants as export hubs as deliberately as Chinese entrants plan to. Plant decisions should be scored on export value, tariff protection and access to public purchasing, not home demand alone. Indian and ASEAN champions can offer contract manufacturing or joint-venture capacity to foreign makers looking for a local base.

Learning 5. Turn local-content and investment rules into an advantage.

Indonesia's revised local-content rules give a product a 25% credit towards its local-content score for investing in a factory in Indonesia, with further credits for local workers and good management. Where a domestic product's combined score exceeds 40%, government buyers must use it and may not import.33 Incumbents hold the advantage today. Komatsu's 20-tonne excavator scores 51.2% on the public e-catalogue (local content plus a weighting for the company's local benefits), while SANY's equivalent shows 0% and is listed as an import.N40 Chinese makers will need local production to close the gap, and LiuGong has committed to raising its local content at Karawang.29

India has moved from blocking Chinese capital to channelling it. Since 2020, investment from countries that share a land border with India has needed government approval. Press Note 2, approved in March 2026, eases this for minority stakes: proposals in capital goods and some related sectors are to be decided within 60 days, provided majority ownership and control stay with resident Indians.34 Legal commentators note that the 60-day timeline may not be binding.35 Other rules still point the other way: since July 2020, firms from those countries may bid in public tenders only after registering with a government committee.126 Chinese capital can now enter India as a minority partner more easily than Chinese-made machines can win public tenders.

Where it stops. Rules now move in both directions. India's tender rules still keep most Chinese machines out of public purchasing, and Indonesia has exempted US products from its local-content rules since February 2026.61

What others can copy. Incumbents should treat local-content thresholds as a route into public purchasing, not a compliance cost; the first to qualify often sets the specification. Indian and ASEAN champions can use Press Note 2 to take Chinese technology and minority capital while keeping control.

Taken together, the six moves are commercial choices, not subsidies. Most are cheaper for incumbents, who already own finance arms, installed bases and local plants, than they were for the Chinese firms that made them first. Chapter 3 asks where the same moves go next.

v1 · Chapter 8. Learning 5: Turn local-content and investment rules into an advantage · Indonesia now rewards

Indonesia's revised local-content rules, Permenperin 35/2025, give a 25% credit towards a product's TKDN score for investing and building a factory in Indonesia, a further 10% for employing local workers and 15% for good management. Where a domestic product exceeds 40%, government buyers must use it and may not import.33 The e-catalogue shows why this matters: Komatsu's PC200-class listings mostly show 51.21%, Hitachi's ZX200 listings 39.35%, and no SANY or XCMG listing in the class shows a score.117 Incumbents still hold the advantage in public purchasing, and Chinese entrants will need local production to close it. LiuGong has committed to raising local components at Karawang.29 Since February 2026, US products have been exempt from the rules.61

v1 · Chapter 8. Learning 5: Turn local-content and investment rules into an advantage · India has moved

Since 2020, investment from countries that share a land border with India has needed government approval.35 Press Note 2 (2026), approved on 10 March, relaxes this. Investors based elsewhere may enter automatically where beneficial ownership from those countries is non-controlling and no more than 10%, and proposals in capital goods, electronic components and some related sectors must be decided within 60 days. The condition is that majority ownership and control stay with resident Indians.34 Legal commentators note that the 60-day timeline may not be binding.35

Other rules point the other way. Since 2020, bidders from land-border countries have been barred from public tenders unless registered and cleared, with sector exemptions.126,127 In September 2026 India's government e-marketplace listed no Chinese brand in crawler excavators, backhoe loaders, forklifts or small mobile cranes.129 The crane duties were recommended but not imposed.37 The net effect is that Chinese capital can now enter India as a minority partner more easily than Chinese-made machines can win public tenders.

Case: Thailand's incentives with obligations

Thailand's EV 3.5 scheme offered subsidies of up to THB100,000 per vehicle, but required two vehicles built locally for each one imported, rising to three in 2027, with fines of about THB400,000 per car for shortfalls.46 Neta's failure under the scheme (Chapter 12) shows the risk of taking incentives without the capacity to meet their obligations.

v1 · Chapter 8. Learning 5: Turn local-content and investment rules into an advantage · Where it stops working

Rules now move in both directions. India's tender rules still keep Chinese machines out of public purchasing, and Indonesia's exemption for US goods changes the calculation only for US-brand OEMs.

v1 · Chapter 8. Learning 5: Turn local-content and investment rules into an advantage · What transfers

  • Global incumbents should model local-content thresholds as a route into public purchasing, not a cost; the first to qualify often sets the specification.
  • Indian and ASEAN champions can use Press Note 2 to take Chinese technology and minority capital while keeping control.
  • Policymakers will get more from these rules by engaging early with firms on both sides.

v1 · Box. Two supporting moves

Building mixed channels. Chinese OEMs combine independent dealers with company-owned outlets and partners. On 6 February 2026 SANY India opened one outlet with an independent dealer in Indore and another under its company-owned model in Thiruvarur.137,138 XCMG's Indonesian dealer, Gaya Makmur Tractors, is now also its finance partner.56 Tata Hitachi, by contrast, makes about 90% of its sales through dealers.42
