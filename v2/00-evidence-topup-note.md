## Needs your decision (it changes how a learning or failure mode is evidenced)

**A. Thai excavators may not be a "loyal installed base holds" case.**
- SANY's Thai distributor says it was Thailand's top excavator seller in 2020 (1,600 units) and 2021 (1,840 units, 30.4% share). This is a company claim from a trade-media report, *search-verified*.
- China's share of Thai excavator imports was already about a third in 2015 and 2019. It reached 42% in 2025.
- So the flat 2019–24 import share probably means Chinese brands arrived earlier, not that Japanese brands held the market.
- **Proposal:**
  - Keep the Thailand verdict ("structural in four of five categories"). It rests on import data and still holds.
  - Stop saying "Japanese brands held Thai excavators" (v1 executive summary, Chapters 12 and 13).
  - Re-evidence failure mode 2 with Tata Hitachi's loyal dealer base and Indian pumps, keeping Thai excavators only as an import-data observation.
  - The failure-mode label is unchanged. Chapter 1 below already uses the neutral wording.
