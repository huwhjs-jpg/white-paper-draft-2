# Evidence top-up: what changed (2 Oct 2026, re-checked after network access was opened)

**How it was checked.** The first pass used web search only. After access was opened, I re-pulled UN Comtrade and read the key sources directly: the HKEX filings, the DGTR findings (the laser case is a scanned file, so it was read by OCR), Hengli's annual report and the news pages. A few sites still block automated reading: INAPROC, Desimachines, Investing.com and electrive. Facts from those sites stay marked *search-verified*.

## Adopted provisionally (they change how a learning or failure mode is evidenced; say if you disagree)

**A. Thai excavators are no longer described as "Japanese brands held".**
- SANY's Thai distributor reports it was Thailand's top excavator seller in 2020 (1,600 units) and 2021 (1,840 units, a 30.4% share). This is a company claim; I read the page.
- China's share of Thai excavator imports was already about a third in 2015, and it was 42% in 2025.
- The Thailand verdict (structural in four of five categories) is unchanged.
- Failure mode 2 is now evidenced mainly with Indian examples.

**B. Indian excavator asking prices are dropped from Learning 3.**
- TractorJunction lists SANY's 21-tonne model at INR 41–43 lakh, against INR 74–76 lakh for Komatsu's PC210 (pages read).
- That gap is too wide, and too dependent on which listing site you use, to support "inside the incumbents' range".
- Chapter 2 relies instead on the regulator's 0–10% undercutting on cranes and on Indonesia's e-catalogue: SANY Rp2.10bn against Komatsu Rp2.25bn, including VAT (*search-verified*).

## Fixed and logged (no verdict or headline change)

1. **Indonesia's robots: explained in part.**
   - The Comtrade re-pull matches the 62% exactly: US$61.5m from China out of US$99.1m in 2025. Japan fell from US$22.2m to US$11.4m.
   - The jump is lumpy. US$51m of the Chinese total arrived in September–December 2025, and US$30m of that in October alone. That fits the fit-out of one or more large plants; the buyer is unconfirmed.
   - Without Indonesia, China rose from 21% to 37% across India, Thailand and Malaysia, while Japan went from 35% to 31%. **The headline holds.**
2. **Coal.**
   - 2025 output was 817.48 Mt (ESDM, page read).
   - The 2026 work-plan (RKAB) target was about 600 Mt, with 580 Mt approved by 27 March (CNBC Indonesia).
   - Lenders were reluctant to lend without approved work plans (Intraco Penta director, *Jakarta Post*, 9 Apr 2026).
   - 95% of the revised plans were approved by 29 September. The Director General's estimate of 2026 output is "in between" 720 Mt (Detik).
   - Write: "quotas first set at about 600 Mt; output expected at about 720 Mt".
3. **Pumps quality-control order.** The May 2025 draft is confirmed (page read). No final order was found.
4. **Atlas Copco's Chinese brands.** Liutech's own site now markets dedicated ranges for India and for Southeast Asia, on "competitive value" (pages read). Nothing was found for Bolaite.
5. **Hengli.** The 2025 annual report shows overseas sales of RMB2,106m (+1.6%) against RMB8,750m at home (+20.7%). That is **about 19% overseas, not "over 35%"**. Appendix B only.
6. **Haitian (HKEX filings read).**
   - The overseas share is **42.9%**, not 42.8%.
   - The filing's English wording credits "the global expansion of Chinese enterprises", with Southeast Asia and South America leading.
   - No country split is given.
   - **New:** the annual report carries an INR1.86bn (RMB146m) provision for an Indian customs anti-dumping claim against Haitian's Indian unit, already present in 2024.
7. **India's laser case (DGTR findings read by OCR).**
   - The case opened on **29 Sep 2022**, not October.
   - The findings are dated 27 Sep 2023 and were posted on 5 Oct 2023. The investigation period ran from Apr 2021 to Mar 2022.
   - **Imports from China supplied 78% of Indian demand in 2018-19 and 83% in 2021-22**, and the applicant held about 3%.
8. **SANY 2022 (page read).** The overseas gross margin was 26.4%, against 21.9% in China. The release does not say "for the first time", so that phrase is dropped.
9. **Indonesia's local-content score.** Komatsu's 51.21% is local content plus a company-benefit weighting (36.2% plus 15). That combined score is the one tested against the 40% bar. SANY's listing shows 0% and is marked as an import (*search-verified*; INAPROC blocks automated reading).
10. **GeM listing.** Not verifiable. Replace it with the July 2020 rule that bidders from countries sharing a land border must register.
11. **Warehouse robots.**
    - No named deployment in India or ASEAN.
    - Hai Robotics opened a factory in Penang in 2025, one of two that raised its output tenfold (page read). This is the first regional "Stay" move for warehouse robots.

**Unchanged:** the governing thought, the central question, the six learnings, the six reader types, the six failure-mode labels, the India and Indonesia verdicts, and the three headline numbers.
